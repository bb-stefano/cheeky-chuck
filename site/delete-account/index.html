Cheeky Chuck

Delete your account

A Cheeky Chuck account holds your email address, a hashed password, your game progress and the photos you sent from the in-game camera. Deleting it removes all of that at once. There is nothing to export first, apart from saving any gallery photo you want to keep.

From inside the game

  1. Open the settings page (the gear button) and switch to the accounts tab.
  2. Sign in if you are not already.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm with your password.

The account, its progress and every photo you sent disappear immediately, from our server and from the public gallery.

By email

If you no longer have the game installed, write to contact@jocogiant.com from the email address of the account, with the subject "Delete my Cheeky Chuck account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply. We may ask you to confirm from that address, which is the only way we can tell the account is yours.

What is not covered

  • Anonymous play statistics are not tied to the account and cannot be looked up per person; see the privacy policy.
  • Emails already delivered to a friend stay in that friend's mailbox, but the photo link in them stops working once the photo is deleted.
