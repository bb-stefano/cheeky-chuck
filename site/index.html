Meet Chuck, your virtual boy

Chuck lives in his little room, and he notices everything. Every touch gets a reaction: a giggle, a grumble, or a slap right back at you. Some are easy to find, others take a bit of mischief.

First released in 2012 and now rebuilt in HD, Cheeky Chuck is free on Android and iPhone.

A look inside Chuck's room

Poke him, tickle him, make him dizzy

  • Touch reactionsTickle him, poke him, punch his belly or his face (he may slap you back), or kick his legs.
  • Shake things upShake your phone to make Chuck dizzy, or stretch him with two fingers.
  • Flips and tricksFlip him into a handstand and see how long he lasts.
  • Lights outSwitch off his light and Chuck goes to sleep. Or call the cat and watch what happens.
  • Dozens to discoverEvery new reaction you find earns a star. Can you collect them all?
  • Camera and galleryFrame a photo of the scene, email it to a friend, and share it in the public gallery.
