Cheeky Chuck

Privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026. Applies to the Cheeky Chuck game for Android, iOS and the web, and to this website (cheekychuck.jocogiant.com), all made and run by Joco Giant.

Cheeky Chuck is a game, not a data business. It shows no advertising, sells nothing, and does not sell or rent any information about you. This page lists the little it does handle, why, and how to get rid of it.

What the game collects

WhatWhyWhere it goes
Anonymous play statistics: a random identifier created on your device (not tied to your name, email, phone number or advertising ID), the events you trigger in the game (opening a scene, a reaction, a discovery), the game version, your device's time zone and language, and the campaign tags of the link that led you to install the game, if the Google Play Store passes them on.To see which parts of the game are used, where players come from and whether an update broke something.Our own analytics server (analytics.bubbleye.com), operated by us. Players are placed on a map by time zone only; we do not look up your location from your IP address.
Your account: email address and password, plus your game progress, if you create an account.An account is needed only to send photos from the in-game camera, and it carries your progress to another device. The game plays fully without one.Our server behind this website. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
Photos you send: the picture framed with the in-game camera, the comment you write, and the time.They are published in the public gallery on this site, and the friend you choose receives a link to them by email. Nothing in the gallery shows your name or email address.Our server behind this website, publicly visible in the gallery until you delete them.
The friend's email address you type when sending a photo.To send that one email. We keep only a one-way hash of the address, used to limit how many photos one address can receive per day and to honour the unsubscribe link in every email.Our mail server. The address itself is not stored.

What the game does not do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no advertising identifier.
  • No access to your contacts, precise location, microphone or camera roll. The in-game camera photographs the game scene, not the world.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no sale or sharing of data for marketing.

Switching statistics off

Open the settings page inside the game and turn off "share anonymous stats". From then on nothing is sent, and whatever was still queued on your device is discarded.

Deleting your data

  • Account and photos: in the game, open the accounts page from the settings and choose "Delete account". This removes the account, its progress and every photo you sent, immediately. You can also ask us to do it.
  • Emails to a friend: every email has an unsubscribe link; once used, that address never receives Cheeky Chuck photos again.
  • Anonymous statistics cannot be linked back to you, so they cannot be looked up or deleted per person. They are kept to compare the game over time.

Children

Cheeky Chuck is meant for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information, such as an email address, from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Where and how long

Everything above is stored on servers we rent from Hetzner in the European Union, and travels encrypted (HTTPS) between the game and our servers. Web server logs, which include IP addresses, are kept for a short time for security and then discarded. Accounts and photos stay until you delete them.

Your rights and how to reach us

You can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to its use, by writing to contact@jocogiant.com. If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new effective date.
